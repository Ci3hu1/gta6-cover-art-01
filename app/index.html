PALM HEAT Cover Studio

Turn any photo + caption into GTA 6-style cover art. Offline, in your browser.

Five different photos, one per panel. Drag nothing — pick a photo per panel, set the caption, export. The comic-ink pipeline (Sobel outlines, 5-tone posterize, halftone shadows, grain) runs before the grade, so every panel reads as hand-inked poster art.

Preview — the export renders at full size (1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080).

Palm Heat Cover Studio — an offline cover maker. Anton & Pacifico fonts under the SIL Open Font License (see app/fonts/OFL.txt). No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or fonts. Photos are bundled samples, free to use.
